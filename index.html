about_edi.md

Hi, I'm Edi. Before AI agents existed, I spent over ten years leading real-world operations teams — and since 2016 I've been building and running Bitcoin infrastructure hands-on.

Today I run the faktotum personal lab: local CPU/GPU rigs, self-hosted LLMs, custom CLIs, and Lightning/RGB tools shipped in the open. No agency layers, no endless slide decks — straight from real constraints to running code.

When you work with me, you get a working product fast and keep full ownership of everything: clean repo, local-first stack, zero mandatory cloud lock-in.
